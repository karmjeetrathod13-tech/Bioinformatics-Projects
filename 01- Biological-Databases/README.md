# Experiment 1: Exploring Biological Databases

## Objective

To explore and use different biological databases for retrieving
biological information relevant to bioinformatics research.

## Databases Used

- NCBI GenBank
- UniProt
- KEGG
- Protein Data Bank (PDB)

## Introduction

Biological databases are organized repositories that store
biological information such as nucleotide sequences, protein
sequences, 3D structures, metabolic pathways, and functional
annotations.

In this experiment, different biological databases were explored
to understand their purpose and retrieve relevant biological data.

## Database Overview

| Database | Major Information |
|----------|-------------------|
| NCBI GenBank | Nucleotide sequences |
| UniProt | Protein sequences and functional information |
| KEGG | Metabolic pathways |
| PDB | 3D structures of biological macromolecules |

## Methodology

1. Explored the NCBI database and searched for nucleotide sequences.
2. Retrieved sequence information and examined accession numbers
   and annotations.
3. Explored UniProt to obtain protein sequence and functional
   information.
4. Explored KEGG to study biological pathways.
5. Examined the information available through the PDB database.

## Results

Different biological databases were successfully explored and
their applications in bioinformatics were studied.

## Conclusion

Biological databases provide organized and accessible biological
information that can be used for sequence analysis, functional
annotation, structural analysis, and pathway studies.
